## Cross-cutting rules the entries keep proving

1. **Stream order is the law** (BUG-006/007/010/016/017/024/026/030): a read
   observes bytes as of ITS stream position; every "mirror", "snapshot",
   "deferred deposit" or cache needs an epoch proof, exact spans, and
   publication ordering.
2. **Game-visible time must be emulated time** (BUG-002/013/014/019/029): any
   host clock reaching guest-visible state is a bug factory — phase, quantum,
   and continuity all matter.
3. **Scale must be unwound around native-pixel semantics** (BUG-008/015/018/022):
   aliasing permutations, UV phase, and z-source reads are defined on native
   GS pixels only.
4. **Enhancement fixes are coarser-only, hatch-0-bit-exact, ledgered**
   (BUG-009/011/012).
5. **Stubs and dropped registers are silent time bombs** (BUG-003/020/021):
   prove "unused" with a census before stubbing.
6. **Measure the chain before believing the symptom** (BUG-025/027): sampling
   profilers and per-stage stamps beat intuition.
