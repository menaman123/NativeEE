| **Root cause** | (a) FIXED: `BlendFfMap::Map` lacked `0122`; GL expresses it exactly as `GL_FUNC_SUBTRACT` with `CONSTANT_ALPHA` on both factors (`Src×FIX − Dst×FIX`). It is a construction gap in the table, not a title mechanism: the title's `[220]` runs `blend=sw` (the render-target self-read route), so the arm changed no title pixel (corrected by P8.21-A; the P8.20-A text that attributed `[220]` to the fallback was wrong). The pinned FF arms are exact on sample pixels (`Render_BlendEquationPin`, textured MODULATE included), so the remaining suspects are the texel ADDRESSING / CONTENT of the decoded textures on the STQ path (UV region rebase, native-centre requantisation, mip LOD floor — the letters are a 32x32 T8 atlas sampled by a 2,080-vertex mesh) and the Z ordering of the glow against the letters (candidate C) |
